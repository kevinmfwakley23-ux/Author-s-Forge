# Mission 007 — Intelligent Editing

Status: implementation complete; awaiting full local verification.

Mission 007 establishes the governed editorial-analysis layer required by the Master Product Directive. It supports ten editorial roles and structured findings for pacing, character consistency, plot holes, continuity conflicts, repetition, weak scenes, unresolved threads, unnecessary exposition, dialogue problems, POV violations, tense inconsistencies, clichés, overused words, sentence rhythm, chapter balance, and genre fit.

Editorial analysis is strictly read-only. Reports carry source ranges, exact excerpts, severity, confidence, recommendations, and deterministic identifiers. They contain no replacement manuscript and cannot authorize manuscript mutation.

The branch intentionally remains separate from `main` pending the complete verification run.
