# Author's Forge — Mission 007

## Intelligent Editing

### Objective
Establish a governed editorial analysis layer that can act as developmental, continuity, line, copy, proofreading, structural, dialogue, pacing, character, and genre editor without silently modifying manuscript content.

### Required editorial findings
The service recognizes and reports pacing, character consistency, plot holes, continuity conflicts, repetition, weak scenes, unresolved threads, unnecessary exposition, dialogue problems, POV violations, tense inconsistencies, clichés, overused words, sentence rhythm, chapter balance, and genre-fit signals.

### Non-negotiable mutation boundary
Editorial analysis is read-only. A report contains source ranges, excerpts, severity, confidence, explanation, and recommendation, but no replacement manuscript and no authorization to mutate manuscript state. Any future author-approved edit must cross an explicit application boundary and use the Writing Engine's content-truth protections.

### Acceptance criteria
- All ten directive editorial roles are represented by explicit types and role routing.
- Reports contain stable identifiers, source ranges, excerpts, severity, confidence, recommendations, and timestamps.
- Findings are deterministic for identical input and configuration.
- Findings are validated against their source excerpts.
- Analysis cannot authorize or silently perform manuscript mutation.
- Repetition, overuse, clichés, sentence rhythm, pacing, dialogue, continuity, character, unresolved-thread, exposition, structural, developmental, and genre signals are represented by concrete analysis rules.
- Invalid targets, roles, ranges, confidence values, and duplicate finding identifiers are rejected.
- The service remains provider-neutral and portable.
