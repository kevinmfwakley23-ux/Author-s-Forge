Agent rules

  • Server-planned. The browser asks Forge for the governed plan instead of inventing one locally.
  • Source-backed research only. Live research refuses to run when its real provider boundary is unavailable.
  • Proposal, not takeover. Writing and Chapter Cards remain candidates until separate author review.
  • Safe groups are bounded. Only read-only/no-state-effect steps can share one approval, and the group stops on the first failure.
  • Artifacts are real. Production downloads the exact bytes returned by Forge.
