# Author's Forge — Product Vision

## Product Identity

Author's Forge is a professional AI authoring studio for creators who want to develop, write, revise, illustrate, package, publish, and market books from one durable workspace.

## Core Promise

The Forge should support long-form creative work without losing canon, character identity, world rules, chronology, voice, or user intent. The system should preserve project continuity across long sessions, long manuscripts, series, pauses, exports, and resumptions.

## Core Product Areas

- Project and book management
- Long-form manuscript generation and revision
- Canon, continuity, character, world, and series memory
- Research and external knowledge access
- Editing, critique, structural review, and quality assurance
- Illustration and character-image development
- Cover and visual identity development
- Publishing preparation and export
- Marketing and launch support
- Journal/question randomization and content-variation tools
- Portable project save, restore, and external backup workflows

## K.I.N.G.S. Relationship

K.I.N.G.S. is the independent intelligence/workforce platform. Author's Forge should integrate with K.I.N.G.S. through explicit, versioned interfaces rather than embedding a duplicate orchestration system.

## Development Rule

Every major capability must be introduced through a bounded mission with executable acceptance criteria and a durable checkpoint. Avoid asking one autonomous run to build the entire product at once.
